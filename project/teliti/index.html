TELITI

TELITI (Teknologi Evaluasi Lowongan dan Integritas) gives a job posting a scam-risk score and shows which phrases pushed it up. It is meant for Indonesian job seekers, yet the model never saw an Indonesian ad in training.

My part
[What you built]
Team
Universitas Gadjah Mada students
Data
EMSCAD (English job ads) for training; 195 real Indonesian ads, labelled twice by hand, for calibration and evaluation
Stack
PyTorch, multilingual DistilBERT, a web API

Overview

  • 0.93PR-AUC on 195 real Indonesian job ads, 36% of them scams
  • 0Indonesian ads used to train the model’s weights
  • 458automated tests in the repository

Fake job ads cost people money and personal data, and they reach the people who can least afford it. Most scam detectors learn from English ads; TELITI tests whether one can carry over to Indonesian ads without Indonesian training data.

Each result comes with its reasons: the phrases that raised the score are highlighted, so a job seeker can see why an ad looks risky instead of trusting a number.

On the English test set a TF-IDF baseline still scores higher (PR-AUC 0.865 against 0.788). The transformer was kept because it carries over to Indonesian.

How it works

  1. Train on English

    A multilingual DistilBERT fine-tuned on EMSCAD, compared with logistic regression, linear SVM and gradient-boosting baselines.

  2. Calibrate for Indonesia

    Indonesian ads never touch the model’s weights. They set the probability calibration and the decision thresholds.

  3. Explain every score

    Occlusion: each phrase is hidden in turn and the change in the model’s margin shows how much it pushed the risk up.

  4. Rules as advice

    Hand-written red-flag rules were tried inside the score, but they raised false alarms, so they are shown as hints and left out of the number.

  5. Serve it

    A web API returns the score and the highlighted phrases for each ad.

Results

PR-AUC on the 195-ad Indonesian holdout. Adding the rule layer to the score lowered it, which is why the rules are advisory only.
