Tuberculosis screening from chest X-rays

Digital Image Processing course, team of four

A classic image-processing pipeline instead of a deep network: enhancement, lung segmentation with Otsu thresholding and morphology, then intensity, histogram and HOG features for an SVM. After tuning it reached 91.9% accuracy on 1,400 held-out X-rays, and a Flask app shows the lung mask and the call for any uploaded image.

  • OpenCV
  • scikit-learn
  • SVM
  • Flask
